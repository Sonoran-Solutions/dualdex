## 1. Engine Battle Mechanics vs. Vanilla Gen III & Bundled Calculator

### 1.1 Overall Damage Pipeline & Rounding Order
- **CFRU Battle Script Pipeline**: Macro `calculatedamage` runs `atk04_critcalc` -> `atk05_damagecalc` -> `atk06_typecalc` -> `atk07_adjustnormaldamage` (**VERIFIED**: `cfru/battle_script_macros.s:54-59`; exact-ROM script bytecode parity **UNVERIFIED**).
- **Step 1 (`atk05_damagecalc`)**: `atk05_damagecalc` (`cfru/src/damage_calc.c:253-297`) fills `struct DamageCalc` via `PopulateDamageCalcStruct` (`:2568-2639`), calls `CalculateBaseDamage` (`:2414-3261`), and multiplies by `gCritMultiplier / BASE_CRIT_MULTIPLIER` (`:281, 290`) (**VERIFIED**: `cfru/src/damage_calc.c:253-297`).
- **Step 2 (`CalculateBaseDamage` internal order)**:
  - `GetBasePower` (`:3299-3846`) and `AdjustBasePower` (`:3848-4209`) compute integer base power with sequential `(bp * num) / denom` truncations (**VERIFIED**: `cfru/src/damage_calc.c:3299-4209`).
  - Stat pass (`:2644-2979`) applies ability stat multipliers (`:2644-2798`), item stat multipliers (`:2801-2900`), optional `BADGE_BOOSTS` (`:2903-2922`), Sandstorm SpD (`:2925-2932`), optional `OLD_EXPLOSION_BOOST` (`:2935-2940`), and stat stages `APPLY_QUICK_STAT_MOD` (`:2943-2979`) (**VERIFIED**: `cfru/src/damage_calc.c:2644-2979`).
  - Core damage `((2 * level / 5 + 2) * power * atk / def) / 50 + 2` is integer-truncated (`:2981-3010`) (**VERIFIED**: `cfru/src/damage_calc.c:2981-3010`).
  - Post-base integer modifiers apply sequentially: move-specific scalers (`:3014-3056`), Me First (`:3059`), Helping Hand (`:3063`), Rain/Sun (`:3067-3091`), Dark/Fairy Aura & Aura Break (`:3094-3103`), Tinted Lens & Sniper (`:3106-3116`), Expert Belt (`:3119-3125`), defender abilities (`:3128-3180`), Friend Guard (`:3183`), Weakness Berries (`:3187-3202`), Screens (`:3205-3215`), Burn/Frostbite (`:3218-3227`), Parental Bond (`:3230-3237`), Doubles spread `×75/100` (`:3240-3248`), and Shadow Shield Battle (`:3252-3255`) (**VERIFIED**: `cfru/src/damage_calc.c:3014-3255`).
- **Step 3 (`atk06_typecalc`)**: Applies STAB (`×15/10`, or `×2` for `ABILITY_ADAPTABILITY`, across `type1`, `type2`, `type3` at `:824-830`) and sequential per-type effectiveness via `TypeDamageModificationByDefTypes` / `ModulateDmgByType` (`:1448-1575`) (**VERIFIED**: `cfru/src/damage_calc.c:795-932, 1448-1575`).
- **Step 4 (`atk07_adjustnormaldamage`)**: Calls `ApplyRandomDmgMultiplier` (`:4420-4425`, multiplying by `100 - (Random() & 15)` in `85..100` with `/ 100` floor and minimum `1`) and `AdjustDamage` (`:2028-2150`, applying Z/Max protect `×25/100`, Raid shield division/clamping, and Sturdy/Focus Sash/False Swipe) (**VERIFIED**: `cfru/src/damage_calc.c:2011-2150, 4420-4425`).
- **Divergence from Bundled `entry.js`**:
  - Bundled `entry.js` wraps `@smogon/calc` `0.11.0` (`Generations.get(input.gen || 3)`) and a custom `hns_2_0_5` UQ4.12 pipeline (`calculateHnsDamage`) (**VERIFIED**: `tools/calc-bundler/entry.js:1-6, 179-358`).
  - `@smogon/calc` Gen 3 lacks Gen 4–8 mechanics and orders weather/screens/burn before `+2` and random roll before/after STAB differently; `@smogon/calc` Gen 8 uses 4096-based pokéround chaining and places Life Orb, Expert Belt, Tinted Lens, and Neuroforce in the final modifier pass after STAB/type/random, whereas CFRU uses base-10/base-100 integer truncations, applies Life Orb to **base power** (`cfru/src/damage_calc.c:4135-4138`), applies Expert Belt/Tinted Lens/Sniper/Screens/Burn *before* crit, STAB, and type effectiveness, and applies the random roll *last* (**VERIFIED**: `cfru/src/damage_calc.c:281, 824-830, 3067-3227, 4135-4138, 4420-4425`, `tools/calc-bundler/entry.js:244-345`).
  - H&S (`calculateHnsDamage`) applies the random roll *before* STAB/type/burn/screens with UQ4.12 rounding and cannot represent CFRU (**VERIFIED**: `tools/calc-bundler/entry.js:303-312`, `app/src/main/java/com/dualdex/calculator/CalcCapabilityPolicy.kt:250-262`).
