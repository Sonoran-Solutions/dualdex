# Produce a legal Heart & Soul 2.0.5 battery save from a FRESH ROM by normal game progression.
#
# No cheats, no RAM writes, no ROM patching: every step below is an ordinary button press that a
# player could make. The script ends by saving in-game (START -> SAVE -> YES) and the caller then
# flushes the core's battery RAM to a .sav (see make-save.sh).
#
# Path driven: copyright -> title -> main menu -> H&S challenge menu -> Oak/H&S speech ->
# naming screen -> bedroom (clock setup) -> downstairs -> New Bark Town -> Elm's lab ->
# starter selection -> nickname -> Elm again -> back outside -> save.
#
# The H&S challenge menu must be exited with R, not A: A cycles the highlighted option's value,
# while R walks the tabs and R on the last tab jumps straight to the save-and-continue path.

# --- boot screens and the challenge menu -------------------------------------
mash 3700
press R 4
wait 30
press R 4
wait 30
press R 4
wait 30
press R 4
wait 30
press R 4
wait 30
press R 4
wait 30
press A 6
wait 60
press A 6
wait 150
mash 700
matrix stage1-after-challenge-menu

# --- bedroom: reach the wall clock, set the time, dismiss the info window ----
walk RIGHT 5
walk UP 2
walk RIGHT 3
walk UP 1
wait 200
# The wall clock installs its own main callback. `await` walks into it and `untilout` answers the
# "Is this the correct time?" prompt with UP+A (so it cannot pick NO) and stops the instant the
# callback changes - pressing A after that would just re-open the clock, because the player is
# standing directly under the wall clock at (10,2).
await 0x0824972D 12
untilout 0x0824972D 12
wait 150
# Turn to face away from the clock so the later B press cannot re-open the clock view.
press DOWN 4
wait 40
press B 6
wait 60
matrix stage2-bedroom-clock-done

# --- downstairs and out of the house ----------------------------------------
# The mom dialogue at (10,4) is a multi-page message; `walk` presses A when a step is blocked.
walk LEFT 2
wait 150
walk DOWN 6
walk LEFT 1
walk DOWN 1
wait 200
matrix stage3-outside-house

# --- New Bark Town -> Elm's lab ---------------------------------------------
walk DOWN 2
walk LEFT 9
walk UP 4
walk LEFT 1
walk UP 1
wait 200
matrix stage4-in-lab

# --- Elm's intro: the scene only releases the player if the Yes/No prompts are answered YES --
walk UP 5
wait 200
escape DOWN 40
matrix stage5-elm-scene-done

# --- choose the left Poké Ball (Chikorita), answer YES, finish the nickname screen ------------
# Step-by-step this mirrors the verified interaction sequence. `spama` (bounded A presses with
# long gaps) is used rather than `mash`: a short-gap A mash does not advance the message printer
# quickly enough and leaves the player locked, and an unbounded mash on a re-triggerable NPC
# dialogue never returns at all.
walk RIGHT 2
press UP 8
wait 40
press A 8
wait 300
press UP 4
wait 20
press A 8
wait 300
spama 10
press START 6
wait 150
press A 8
wait 250
spama 6
escape DOWN 20
walk LEFT 2
walk UP 2
press UP 4
wait 20
press A 8
wait 250
spama 16
matrix stage7-elm-second-talk
walk DOWN 9
wait 250
matrix stage8-outside-lab

# --- save in-game ------------------------------------------------------------
press START 6
wait 200
matrix stage9-start-menu-open
press DOWN 4
wait 25
press DOWN 4
wait 25
press DOWN 4
wait 25
press DOWN 4
wait 25
press A 8
wait 300
matrix stage10-save-selected
press UP 4
wait 20
press A 8
wait 400
matrix stage11-saved
