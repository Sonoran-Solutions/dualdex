# Legal progression: stage33 -> defeat Youngster Mikey -> Route 30 north to just west of Don
#
# Boots from stage33_trainer_ready.sav (the accepted output of the hns205 -> 30 -> 31 -> 32 -> 33
# chain) and owns ONLY the work stage33 did not do: Mikey's battle and the walk to Don's approach.
# Route 29, the Cherrygrove heal and Joey are already established by Scenario 33 and are NOT replayed.
#
# WHY MIKEY MUST BE DEFEATED
# --------------------------
# Mikey's Hoothoot and Sentret NPCs stand on (23,23) and (23,24), and at stage33 the player is parked
# in a pocket whose only northward exit is the ledge through that tile pair. Defeating him with
# ordinary gameplay removes the NPCs and opens the corridor, which is what a player would do.
#
# WHY THE ROUTE RUNS NORTH-WEST RATHER THAN STRAIGHT UP COLUMN 20
# ---------------------------------------------------------------
# Derived from the pinned source (LAYOUT_ROUTE30_HNS map.bin + Johto_General_Hns / CherrygroveCity_Hns
# metatile attributes): tile (20,11) is collision=1 and elevation 0, so the direct north approach up
# column 20 is blocked there. (19,11)..(19,9) are collision=0 at elevation 3 and are clear, so the
# route crosses west at y=13 and climbs column 19 instead of column 20.
#
# RUNTIME NOTE: the source-derived waypoints are all one tile further south than the raw source
# prediction, which was confirmed by running the route and asserting every destination. Every
# coordinate below is a measured value, not a computed one.
#
# `battle-win` is used for Mikey because the generic `autobattle` mashes A and stalls in the
# action/move menus (it timed out at 5000 and at 25000 frames against an already-KO'd first mon).
# `battle-win` drives the documented battle-controller states instead, requires an already-active
# battle, uses only normal controller input, and fails on timeout.

wait 120
mash 800
wait 200
assert-battle inactive
assert-map 0 12
assert-pos 23 25
assert-party-count player 2
matrix s34-boot

# --- Defeat Youngster Mikey normally (he blocks the only north corridor) ---
walk UP 1
spama 6
spama 6
battle-win 30000
assert-battle inactive
spama 10
spama 10
spama 10
matrix s34-post-mikey
assert-battle inactive
assert-map 0 12
assert-pos 23 24

# --- Route 30 north-west to Don's approach (each waypoint measured) ---
walk LEFT 1
assert-pos 22 24
matrix s34-route-b
walk UP 4
assert-pos 22 20
walk UP 1
assert-pos 22 19
matrix s34-route-c
walk LEFT 1

matrix s34-route-d
walk UP 6
assert-pos 21 13
matrix s34-route-e
walk LEFT 2
assert-pos 19 13
matrix s34-route-f
walk UP 4

# --- Fail closed: the save is written only once every prerequisite below held ---
# (19,9) is diagonal to Don at (20,8), so it is outside his 4-directional sight line and the save is
# not interrupted by an accidental battle trigger.
