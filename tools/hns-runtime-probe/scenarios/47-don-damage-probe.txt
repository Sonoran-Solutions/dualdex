# Issue #1 diagnostic: bounded Razor Leaf damage measurement against Don's Ledyba Lv3.
#
# This scenario asserts NOTHING about switching. It exists to check the source-derived damage
# prediction (3-4 HP of Ledyba's 15 per Razor Leaf hit) against the ROM, because an earlier
# rejection of Don rested on a single observation of Razor Leaf appearing to one-shot Ledyba.
#
# `damage-probe 75 4` drives four turns with move id 75 (Razor Leaf), located in the LIVE move list
# rather than an assumed menu slot, and prints the opponent's HP after every change. It reports
# critical-hit status as UNKNOWN: the core does not read gSpecialStatuses, and adding a speculative
# address purely to detect a crit is out of bounds for this tool.
#
# Ordinary controller input only. No RAM writes, no savestates, no RNG manipulation.

wait 120
mash 800
wait 200
assert-battle inactive
assert-map 0 12
assert-pos 19 9
assert-party-count player 2
party-stats player
matrix dp-boot

walk RIGHT 1 optional
wait 90
engage UP 8
wait 60
assert-battle active
assert-battle-kind TRAINER_SINGLE
assert-party-count enemy 2
assert-active-enemy-slot 0
party-stats enemy
matrix dp-engaged

damage-probe 75 4
matrix dp-after
